## About

I'm a software engineer and technical founder focused on the intersection of
industrial systems and modern cloud infrastructure.

I founded **SCADALGERIA**, where I lead the product and technical development
of a cloud-native SCADA and Industrial IoT platform for real-time industrial
supervision.

My work spans software architecture, .NET backend engineering, distributed
systems, real-time data, edge-to-cloud communication, cloud infrastructure,
DevOps, security, observability, and platform reliability.

---

## What I'm Building

**[SCADALGERIA](https://scadalgeria.com)** is a cloud-native SCADA and IIoT
platform designed to connect industrial systems and bring live telemetry,
alarms, operational history, HMI supervision, and supervisory actions into
one browser-based workspace.

The platform is currently under active development toward its first public
release.

## Background

Before founding SCADALGERIA, I worked as a **SCADA Software Engineer (.NET)**
at DEO ELECTRONIQUE, contributing to the development and modernization of
industrial SCADA software from legacy .NET systems toward a modern architecture.

I also conducted R&D in autonomous mobile robotics and motion planning at the
**Centre de Développement des Technologies Avancées (CDTA)**.

I hold a Master's degree in Embedded Systems Electronics from
Université Saad Dahlab de Blida 1.
