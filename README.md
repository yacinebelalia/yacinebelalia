## 🏭 What is SCADAlgeria?
**[SCADAlgeria](https://www.scadalgeria.com)** is a **cloud-native Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) and IoT platform** delivered as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS). 

We are building the digital nervous system for physical operations. Whether you are an engineering student wiring up an ESP32 microcontroller or an industrial enterprise managing a 50,000-tag production line, SCADAlgeria provides the infrastructure to monitor, control, and analyze physical assets in real-time.

**Why developers & engineers love what we're building:**
*   **Hardware Agnostic:** Connects to anything from legacy PLCs to modern IoT devices.
*   **Cloud-Native & Event-Driven:** Built on a modern microservices architecture designed for massive horizontal scale, high-throughput MQTT ingestion, and sub-millisecond latency.
*   **Real-Time Control:** Bi-directional telemetry and actuation delivered at 60fps to modern, WebGL-accelerated web dashboards.
*   **Data Sovereignty & Security:** Engineered with Zero-Trust principles, cryptographic audit ledgers (WORM), and strict data governance.
